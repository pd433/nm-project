Farm Management System

Digitalizing Agricultural Data Management

A Salesforce-based Farm Management System designed to manage farms, crops, farmers and buyers while providing reports, dashboards, validation and secure role-based access.

📄 View Documentation ▶ View Demo

Project Overview

A centralized platform for modern farm management

🎯 Problem

Agricultural information may be maintained manually through registers, files and spreadsheets, making data management, reporting and monitoring difficult.

💡 Solution

The Farm Management System provides centralized Salesforce-based data management for farms, crops, farmers and buyers.

📊 Impact

The system supports organized data management, validation, reporting, dashboards and data-driven farm management.

Key Features

Main capabilities of the Farm Management System

🌾

Farm Management

Maintain centralized farm information and related agricultural data.

🌱

Crop Management

Manage crop details, planting dates, harvest information and yield data.

👨‍🌾

Farmer Management

Store and manage farmer information through structured records.

🛒

Buyer Management

Maintain buyer contact, email, address and buyer type information.

✅

Data Validation

Validation rules help prevent incorrect or incomplete records.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Profiles, roles and sharing rules support controlled access to system information.

📈

Reports

Generate reports to analyze farm, crop and buyer information.

📊

Dashboards

Visualize important agricultural information using dashboards.
